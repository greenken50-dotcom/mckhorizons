Compliance Governance Framework

A continuous governance system for multi-site petroleum compliance.

A structured system for managing CUPA, EHS, Air Quality, and UST regulatory compliance across multi-state portfolios — ensuring 100% test-frequency adherence and audit readiness.

Core principle

Compliance is not a calendar event — it is a continuous governance system. Every regulatory deadline, inspection, certification, and permit renewal must be tracked, owned, and completed proactively. This framework eliminates the compliance variability that creates CUPA enforcement exposure, operational shutdowns, and corrective-action costs across multi-site portfolios.

01Compliance calendar architecture — 4-tier frequency system

Monthly
  • ATG tank monitoring system inspection
  • Designated Operator site visit
  • Hazardous waste storage & spill-kit inventory / SOP review
  • Emergency response plan awareness
  • Dispenser leak check
Quarterly
  • Leak detection functionality test
  • Overfill / spill prevention visual inspection
  • EHS compliance self-audit
  • Vendor compliance documentation review
  • Stormwater inspection (SWPPP)
Semi-annual
  • UST sump visual inspection
  • Vapor recovery testing (100K throughput)
  • Staff safety training verification
  • Fire suppression system functionality review
  • Water reclamation system service
Annual
  • UST compliance testing (Air Quality, CUPA/EHS)
  • Dispenser meter calibration & filter change
  • ATG monitor certification
  • Hazardous Materials Business Plan review
  • Permit to operate renewal

02Risk tracking & escalation protocol — 4-stage system

60 days out

Automated alert sent to site manager and regional compliance lead. Pre-inspection checklist deployed to field team.

30 days out

Contractor or agency appointment confirmed. Readiness documentation submitted to compliance manager for review.

14 days out

Final readiness verification. Any open items escalated to regional director. No-fail window for last-minute remediation.

Overdue

Immediate VP-level escalation. Emergency contractor dispatch. Regulatory agency notification protocol initiated if required.

03Agency interaction procedures — by regulatory body

CUPA — Certified Unified Program Agency
  • Pre-inspection walk 30 days before scheduled audit
  • Designate a single site contact for all inspector interactions
  • All documentation binders pre-staged at site
  • Finding response within 10 business days — no exceptions
  • Notice of Violation escalated to VP within 24 hours
APCD — Air Pollution Control District
  • Annual permit renewal initiated 90 days before expiration
  • Vapor recovery equipment inspection on PM schedule
  • CARB Phase II compliance verification at each dispenser
  • Annual emissions reporting filed before district deadlines
  • Equipment modification permits tracked in compliance log
EHS — Environmental Health & Safety
  • Annual self-audit against EHS master checklist
  • Safety incident log reviewed and filed monthly
  • Hazardous materials inventory updated semi-annually
  • HMBP site plan submitted to agency on renewal cycle
  • Corrective-action tracking — open items reviewed weekly

05Audit readiness checklist — pre-inspection protocol

60 days before inspection

  • All compliance test records current and filed on-site
  • ATG monitoring logs printed and organized by month
  • Equipment maintenance records for prior 12 months compiled
  • Open corrective-action items identified — remediation started
  • Site manager briefed on inspection scope and agency focus areas
  • Pre-inspection walk conducted with regional compliance lead

Day of inspection

  • Documentation binder staged at front counter — organized by category
  • Designated site contact available for the full inspection duration
  • All areas clean, labeled, and accessible for inspector walk
  • VP / compliance manager on standby for escalated questions
  • Any verbal findings noted — written response committed within 10 days
  • Post-inspection debrief completed same day — findings logged
