The challenge

Margin erosion that hides until it shows up as an EBITDA miss.

Petroleum operators face four compounding pressures that quietly compress margins — often undetected until the numbers slip.

Rising Maintenance Costs

Reactive repairs, delayed response, poorly structured vendor contracts, and untracked R&M spend quietly erode margins — often undetected until they surface as EBITDA misses.

Compliance Complexity

Escalating CUPA, EHS, and Air Quality requirements demand greater resource allocation to sustain compliance and minimize corrective-action risk across jurisdictions.

Workforce & Contractor Gaps

Owners and contractors operate without standardized KPIs, utilization metrics, or accountability frameworks — leaving performance ungoverned and profitability exposed.

Capital Program Inefficiency

Uncontrolled capital spend, unplanned scope deferrals, and budget overruns that compress margins and erode projected ROI on fueling infrastructure.

MCK Horizons bridges this gap — bringing strategic oversight, operational integrity, and a proven track record of margin improvement.
